# Overlays

## Status Monitor
Persistent counters show incident points and track cuts. The time monitor
shows accumulated penalty seconds and stays hidden when there are none.
Points, cuts and seconds are different quantities.

## Race Control Banner
A temporary banner announces warnings, issued sanctions and served penalties.
A banner disappearing does not mean a pending penalty has been served.

## Compliance Advisory
Displays the action you must take and the relevant countdown: throttle
restriction after a cut, laps remaining to serve a penalty, or time stopped
for Stop & Go. Follow the instruction, not only its color.

## Native HUD
Disqualification may also be enforced through the game's native controls.
Other Avenex sanctions use the app's own indicators.

Visibility is controlled by your server and local settings. Hiding an overlay
does not cancel a penalty. See [penalties](../rules-and-sanctions.md) and the
[indicator reference](gallery.md).
